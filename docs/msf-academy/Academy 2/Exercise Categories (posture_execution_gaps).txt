Exercise Categories (based on posture and/or execution)
A. Hand-offs (Intrapersonal Load Transfer):
Compound Row
Pulldown
Pullover
Abdominals
Cervical Extension
- All other movements start from a dead stop with a gradual load up.  Cervical Ext is also the only exercise that the instructor will take from the client in the neutral position when finished.
B. Continuous movement with no pause or squeeze:
Leg Press
Chest Press
Overhead Press
Seated Dip
- All other exercises have a “squeeze” or brief pause in the contracted position.  These exercises also require the instructor to time their ‘click’ to the movement rather than the client waiting for the ‘click’.
C. Finishing a set with a static - For those subjects that do not reach apparent concentric failure, we will often end the set with an attempt to achieve a higher level of intensity.  This is usually in the form of a static hold in a leveraged but still challenging position where the instructor will encourage them to keep working while counting them down.  On the following four compound pushing movements (also continuous as indicated above) it is best to have the subject perform their last complete or nearly complete repetition, and then at the approximate midpoint of the final eccentric have them pause.
Leg Press
Chest Press
Overhead Press
Seated Dip
A count of 5 to 10 seconds is usually performed here while the subject works to hold their midpoint position.  Again, this is in lieu of the preferred course of training to concentric failure.  Importantly, all other movements outside of the four listed above, are usually best intensified by having the subject hold the last rep in the contracted position while the instructor counts them down.  The only exceptions would be the Cervical and Lumbar Extensions.  A normal 2 to 3 second count in the contracted position makes more sense for these two movements because we are not seeking a high level of intensity with them.
D. Posterior Pelvic Tilt (contracted abdomen):
Overhead Press
Lateral Raise
Abdominals
Pullover
Seated Dip
Torso Rotation
- All other exercises are performed with a “chest up” posture.
E. Gaps - Machines and Standard Gaps (standard essentially means “starting point”-we may determine some clients need more or less of a gap based on experience, mobility, and tolerance).  Keep in mind, gaps may vary from location to location because of difference in manufacturing and/or installation:
Lumbar - (4 to 6)
Abdominals - (5 to 7)
Leg Curl - (2 or 3)
Leg Extension - (2 or 3)
Pulldown - 2 (more if shorter client with a high seat; avoid using seats 1-4)
Overhead - 2
Pullover - (6 or 7)
Biceps - 2
Seated Dip (use gap for shorter clients with a high seat; avoid using seats 1-4)
All other machines either have a custom gap or no gap.  Please refer to the Equipment Scripts(MSF Equipment Setup and Instruction Procedures) for more information.
