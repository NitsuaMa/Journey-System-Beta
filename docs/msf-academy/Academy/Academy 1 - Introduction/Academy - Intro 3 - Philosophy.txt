Introduction
Our Philosophy
The exercise protocol and methods used at MaxStrength Fitness are an evolution of the Nautilus high-intensity training principles of Arthur Jones, the inventor of Nautilus exercise equipment.  Of the various conflicting exercise theories and fads that have come and gone over the years, the principles of high-intensity training remain the most consistent with scientific research and the understanding of physiology.  Accordingly, MSF’s approach to resistance training incorporates the most relevant literature that aligns with time-proven, fundamental exercise principles and physiological outcomes .
As you proceed through this manual, you will be introduced to the theory and principles of our Continuous Tension Method, the exercise protocol used at MaxStrength Fitness. You will also learn how to apply them in the design and instruction of effective, efficient, and safe exercise programs.  Developing a thorough understanding of the material in this manual, in addition to the experience gained from your attention to detail over time, will ultimately lead you to become a master level, highly valued and sought after, strength training professional.  While your first read through this conceptual framework may leave you with a multitude of questions, trust that this will all begin to come together as you learn the application side of our profession.
