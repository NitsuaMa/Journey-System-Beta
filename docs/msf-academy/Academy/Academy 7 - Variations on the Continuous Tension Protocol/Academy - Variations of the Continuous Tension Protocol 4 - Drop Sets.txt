Variations of the Continuous Tension Protocol
Drop Sets
A drop set is used to continue the set if a longer duration is desired, as may be the case if the initial resistance selection was too heavy resulting in a very short duration or poor execution.  A drop set is performed by briefly pausing to reduce the weight by an appropriate amount after the maximum tolerable perception of effort has been reached to enable the trainee to continue.  Importantly, the quality and effect of a workout is entirely up to the instructor to enforce and ensure. Because of this, if the client is struggling to control the weight or they do not feel good about their performance, it will be the instructor's responsibility to make an adjustment and ensure that they themselves and the client are satisfied with each and every set of exercise. While we do not want to get in the habit of having to constantly adjust weights and in a sense "try again," we should do everything in our power to ensure client satisfaction.
