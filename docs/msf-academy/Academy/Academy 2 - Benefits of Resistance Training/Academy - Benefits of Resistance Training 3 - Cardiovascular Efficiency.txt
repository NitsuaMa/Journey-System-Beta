Benefits of Resistance Training
Cardiovascular Efficiency
The cardiovascular system supports muscular work by delivering oxygen and nutrients to, and carrying carbon dioxide and other metabolic wastes away, from the muscles.  Improvements in cardiovascular efficiency enable you to perform more demanding physical activities longer and with less fatigue.
Any activity that places a significant demand on the musculature will place a significant demand on the cardiovascular system (and all other subsystems in the body).  This demand on the cardiovascular system is what stimulates improvements in cardiovascular efficiency.  Resistance training improves cardiovascular efficiency without the time commitment and high risk of injury associated with traditional aerobic activities, typically in the form of repetitive overuse or impact based movements.
When resistance training is performed with meaningful mechanical tension and rest intervals between exercises are limited, the demand on the cardiovascular system increases.  Improvements in the efficiency of metabolic processes that occur from repeated bouts of demanding muscular work, allow the muscles to use the oxygen and nutrients delivered to them by the cardiovascular system more effectively over time.
Importantly, MSF still advocates living an active lifestyle that would also include daily, regular movement. Walking is extremely valuable and has many positive benefits associated with it, as are other recreational activities. Proper strength training should afford one to live a more active life and thus experience all of the additive benefits that come from it. As such, resistance training should be seen as the foundation of a fitness program.
