Benefits of Resistance Training
Muscular Strength, Size and Endurance
Heterotrophic life requires movement, and all movement depends on the strength and function of your muscles.  All other factors of fitness are secondary and serve to support or enable the work being performed by the muscles.  The stronger you are, the easier it is for you to perform any type of physical activity, from walking up a flight of stairs to carrying groceries into the house to hitting a golf ball.  The only effective and sustainable way to improve muscular strength is through proper resistance training.  Other activities do not provide the necessary mechanical tension or progressive challenge to the muscles in the manner biologically required to stimulate strength increases. Simply moving or even moving against a minimal resistance is not enough to be stimulative, unless the organism is severely deconditioned.
Increasing muscular size and density positively affects metabolic rate, the body's ability to control blood sugar levels, and regulate its temperature.  Added muscle tissue also slows the aging process, strengthens the immune system, improves energy levels, and protects the body against physical injury.
Additionally, improving muscular strength results in greater muscular endurance.  Sub-maximal strength increases in accordance with maximum strength. For example, a person who wants to be able to bench press 200 lbs 12 times must first be able to bench press 200 lbs one time.
