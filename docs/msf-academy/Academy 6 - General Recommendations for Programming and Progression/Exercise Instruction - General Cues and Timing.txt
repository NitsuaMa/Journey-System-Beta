To begin the exercise (gradual load up - pushing movement)
When you go to begin the exercise you should gradually add pressure at a
level so minute that you know it will not move at first. Be patient and begin to
build your force incrementally until you eventually break inertia and the
machine begins to move. It should take several or more seconds for this to
happen. [Please see the individual exercise scripts on how it may be
necessary to give more instruction on a gradual load up].
Imagine that there’s 100 pounds on the weight stack and you are trying to find
exactly 101 pounds of pressure to get started.

push to begin the movement.
